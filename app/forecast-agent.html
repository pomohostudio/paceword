Forecast Agent — Forecasting Class 001

Forecast
Agent

Ask about any future event in plain words. It reframes your question into something resolvable, tells you what it thinks and why, gives a probability with a 90% interval, and then keeps talking — push back, ask what would change its mind, stress-test the deadline. Outside view first, increments in log-odds, every number logged and scored on resolution. Live retrieval through Tavily, Exa and Firecrawl, macro data from Alpha Vantage on economic questions; absent those it reasons from model knowledge and says so.
