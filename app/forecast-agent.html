Forecast Agent

Ask about any future event. You get a probability, a 90% interval, and the reasoning — outside view first, increments in log-odds, cruxes named. Every forecast is logged and can be scored when it resolves. With your Tavily/Exa/Firecrawl connectors attached, every run also performs live retrieval; without them it reasons from model knowledge plus whatever evidence you paste, and says so.
